GADGET SALES ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

A well-detailed Gadget Sales Analysis Dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI, designed to visualize and analyze sales performance across different regions, brands, gender, and income levels.

FEATURES

-  Interactive charts and visualizations
-  Slicers (Region, Gender, Color)
-  Profit analysis by Brand and Income Level
-  Monthly customer trend tracking
-  Yearly revenue overview

  INSIGHT FROM THE DASHBOARD

* Apple leads in profit among all brands
* White colored gadgets have the highest total purchases at 1.22M
* Medium income customers contribute the most to profit
* Customer count peaks in March
